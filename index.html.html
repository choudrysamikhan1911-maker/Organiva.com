How to Use ORGNIVA Hair Oil

3 Easy Steps for Healthy, Nourished Hair

STEP 01

Apply

Dispense 3–5 drops onto your palms. Focus on scalp and hair roots, distributing evenly from root to tip.

STEP 02

Massage

Gently massage into your scalp using fingertips in circular motions for 2–3 minutes. This improves circulation.

STEP 03

Leave Overnight

Leave the oil overnight for deep nourishment and repair. Rinse with mild shampoo the next morning.

✅ Pro Tip: Use 2–3 times weekly for thicker, shinier hair
